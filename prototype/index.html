Method, and what we refuse to claim

  • All data is user-reported. We never scrape platforms, and we have no affiliation with them.
  • We never show a statistic below n = 30. Below that we say "insufficient data" and mean it.
  • We report distributions, not predictions. Median, p25–p75, p90 — with sample size attached.
  • We never claim a platform is "slow on purpose." We report what users experienced, nothing more.
  • This is not a promise about your submission. A queue is a distribution; you are one draw from it.

Demo data is synthetic and labelled as such in data/seed.json. Replace it with a real submissions table before showing anyone.
