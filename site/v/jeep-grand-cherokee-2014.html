Methodology. SACR = Σ(severity-weighted complaints) ÷ vehicle-years of exposure × 1,000. Severity weights: crash/fire/injury 5, powertrain failure 3, electrical failure 3, minor 1. Exposure = sum of annual registrations across the model-year's life (FHWA).

Raw complaint counts are not comparable across vehicles: a high-volume model will always accumulate more complaints than a low-volume one. Normalizing by exposure is the whole point.
